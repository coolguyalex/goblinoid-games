How to play

Skip class. Don't get caught.

Goal

  • 🔔 Slip out with the crowd at the bell and stay out until the next one.
  • ❌ Get caught three times and your run is over.
  • 🏁 Get through six periods to finish the day. Each day is harder.

Stay hidden

  • 👀 Stay out of the staff's vision cones. If you're spotted, break line of sight, then hide.
  • 🚪 Walk into a locker, restroom, closet or classroom to hide. If it glows, you can use it.
  • 🎲 Classrooms are a gamble: the teacher might spot you on the way in.
  • 🪜 The green STAIRS doors take you to the other floor.

Make trouble

  • 🎒 Find items in lockers, classrooms and closets.
  • 🎯 Hit kids and staff for points. Staff are worth more.
  • ✨ Your points multiply while nobody's watching you.
  • 📜 The kid in the restroom hands out dares. ✋ Friends in white tees give high fives and trade gum.

Watch out

  • 🔥 HEAT means they know it's you. It drops at every bell. Max it out and you're on a rebel run: double points, but the next catch ends it.
  • 🚨 SCHOOL ALERT brings more guards. It only drops overnight.

Your kid

  • 🧑 On the title screen: 🥷 Stealth, 👟 Speed and 💪 Strength (10 is average), plus your look.
