# Hallway Skip

A three.js browser game (mobile first, landscape): slip out with the crowd, then stay out of class until the next bell.

Built from `Hallway_Skip_Game_Spec` v0.2. Done so far:

- **Step 1:** one hallway, joystick, a hall monitor that chases, a crowd that thins out, and the bell.
- **Step 2:** tap-to-interact and hiding spots. Green lockers (3 uses per period, 15 s each), two restrooms (45 s, but a monitor sweeps on a schedule), and classrooms (a teacher boots you in 4 s, a sub in 14 s; 1–2 subs per day, shown by a green window and a sticky note). If a chasing monitor sees you duck in, they yank you back out.
- **Step 3:** a security guard (narrow, long vision; roams; sprints faster than you for about 5 s, then gets winded; radios for backup), the three-strike ladder (warning → the office, which costs this period's points and puts staff on high alert for the day → run over), and a PA announcer with placeholder lines in `PA_LINES`.
- **Step 4:** a figure-8 floor plan (outer loop plus a main hall across the middle; monitors patrol different loops), the six-period day (more monitors, guards, and teachers at their doors as the day goes on), harder days (faster, sharper staff; shorter passing and longer skipping periods; `CFG.days`), an end-of-day screen with a bonus, and saving at every bell (Continue on the title screen).
- **Step 5:** a four-slot hotbar (right edge), tap-then-flick throwing with a dotted arc preview (the game slows while you aim), and seven items found lying in the halls: paper airplane (points per kid it flies over), spitball (3 shots), banana peel (anyone who steps on it slips; staff go down for 2.5 s), whoopee cushion (noise that pulls staff over), basketball (bounces; each bounce turns staff to look), wheelie chair (launches you down the hall; hitting staff is a catch), and gum (instant havoc points). Repeating the same havoc in one period is worth less each time.
- **Step 6:** rooms as shops. Each room type has a loot table (`ROOM_TYPES`: classroom, science lab, art room, gym, main office). While hiding in a room, tap an item on the desk to grab it; each grab eats 1.5 s of hiding time (0.75 s with a sub), and the teacher may call security (30% per grab; subs 5%; the secretary 50%), sending a guard to the door. Sub rooms have an extra item. The main office has a once-a-day 300-point prize. Desks restock every period; items lying in the halls vanish after 45 s.
- **Step 7:** two-layer heat. HEAT ("they know it's me", 0-100) rises when staff see you doing havoc or start chasing you, flows in at most 30/s, drops 60% at every bell, and cools while you hide; it makes staff faster and sharper, and at 50+ staff can detain you during passing periods (below that, a catch during passing just shoos you, no strike). SCHOOL ALERT ("they know something happened") rises with loud havoc even unseen (more if seen), only drops overnight (-40%), adds an extra guard at 50, and ending a day at 100 makes the next day the LAST DAY: 3 periods, 2× points, max staff; survive it and the run ends as a legend.
- **Step 8:** the principal (walks the halls after strike 2; slow, sees far, and spotting you summons every staff member), friends (white tees) who raise a hand during passing periods: high five (+75), gum trade (your gum for a random item), or a fake pass that cancels your next catch; kids tossing paper airplanes you can catch; and risk scoring: unseen streak (+0.25× per 15 s, up to 3×), near miss (staff within 2.2 m who don't see you: 2× for 5 s), and points for hiding right next to staff.
- **Step 9:** humor pass (staff speech bubbles, squinting monitors, rotating caught-scene and office lines, excuses, tomorrow's lunch; all in the humor tables near the top of `game.js` for rewriting) and ad hooks. Ads use a placeholder screen for now (`CFG.ads.provider`): at most one interstitial per day, only after a strike or between days, never mid-run; optional rewarded ads to forge a note (erase a strike, once a day) or start tomorrow with a fake pass.
- **Step 10:** thrown things that hit someone score, and hitting staff scores more (`hit` in `ITEM_TYPES`). Spitballs and paper airplanes make people stagger; basketballs knock them down and keep bouncing. The paper airplane flies in two stages: high over heads (fly-over points), then low at head height where it can hit someone (blue and orange dots in the aim preview). Whoopee cushions go off when someone steps on them (you too), not when they land. Banana peels stay down after the first slip: they vanish 10 s later or after 5 slips.
- **Step 11:** football (gym; flies farther, every bounce goes somewhere random), stink bomb (science labs; pops on landing, then points for everyone who walks through the cloud), and spray paint (art rooms; tags the nearest wall with random graffiti from `GRAFFITI`, scrubbed off overnight).
- **Step 12:** grab anything from a room and it's locked for the next 2 periods, with no restock (a red sign on the door). The main office now has three once-a-day specials: the 300-point drawer, a fake hall pass, and the master key (3 uses; opens locked or already-used doors, not during a fire alarm). Friends no longer hand out fake passes for free: a gum trade pays 150 points and a fake pass (or an item if you already have one).
- **Step 13:** survival mode replaces the Last Day. Anything that maxes your HEAT starts it: heat stays maxed, more staff arrive every 8 s and get radioed your rough location, points are doubled, and the next catch ends the run. The fire alarm (three red boxes; tap twice to pull, once a day) starts it on purpose: 500 points, every staff member stunned for 3 s, every kid floods the halls, 8 items spill out and stay, and every classroom locks while it rings. Flooding a bathroom (button while hiding in one) is 400 points and a lot of heat, and closes it for the day. The final screen depends on your heat: run over, suspended, or expelled (military school / juvenile hall). SCHOOL ALERT now just adds guards (one at 50, two at 100).
- **Step 14:** keyboard for everything. Keyboard players get faint hints: an "E" bubble on whatever E will do (pick up, hide, high five, trade, catch, fire alarm), numbers on the hotbar, and numbers on the desk items while hiding. 1–4 picks an item, the arrow keys aim (←/→ direction, ↑/↓ distance), Space throws. Sprint: hold Shift (🏃 button on phones); about 3 s of stamina, staff notice a running kid faster, and barging through kids makes them stagger and turns staff heads.
- **Step 15:** a bigger crowd (110 kids, `?crowd=N` in the URL to try 20–250). Kids you can't see (behind you, behind a wall, or in the fog) still move but aren't drawn.
- **Step 15b:** fix: the whole crowd no longer vanishes at some camera angles. Banana peels are thrown about half as far.
- **Step 16:** 7 hiding lockers instead of 13, and each can be rummaged once per period for a blind grab from `CFG.lockers.loot` (about a third of it is junk: a sock, a moldy sandwich, crumpled homework; select junk to toss it). Nothing spawns in the halls any more: items come from rooms, lockers and restrooms. Anything lying around stays until a staff member sees it and walks over to tidy it up (`CFG.tidy`). Traps (banana peels, whoopee cushions) aren't noticed until they go off, and whoever set one off picks it up once they're back on their feet, so a whoopee cushion can't go off under the same person over and over. At most 24 things lie around at once. Balls (and frisbees) only come from the gym.
- **Step 17:** slime (science labs; the first 3 people to step in the puddle are stuck in place: staff can still look and yell, they just can't move), toilet paper (restrooms, "Grab a roll" while hiding; flies like the paper airplane and leaves a streamer down the hall that stays up until the next morning, with points per meter), and frisbee (gym; the same two-stage flight but stays up about twice as long, and you can pick it back up).
- **Step 18:** no more restroom sweep timer. When staff walk past a restroom they stick their head in about half the time (`CFG.hiding.restroomCheck`): "Hey, do you kids have a pass?" If you're inside you get a warning as they walk over, then they find you and chase you.
- **Step 19:** dares. A kid by the restroom sinks offers one per visit (📜 on the restroom sign); you can hold two (`CFG.dares`). They're data in `DARES`: hit a hall monitor with 3 spitballs, trade gum, deliver a love note from a science lab to the gym in a given period, tag every hallway, flood a bathroom in a given period, fly something over 10 kids in one throw, raid every room on one hall in a day, hit staff with a basketball, spitball and banana peel in one period, banana-slip a staff member who's chasing you, lure a hall monitor onto a whoopee cushion, slime a guard, get through a skipping period without being chased, leave a 15 m toilet-paper trail, and steal from the main office. Dares tied to a period fail when it's over, and dares save with your run.

## Before turning on real ads

AdSense itself doesn't allow ads inside games; game ads go through a games ad network (for example Google's H5 Games Ads `adBreak` API, or a games portal SDK), which plugs into `Ads.show()`. Because the game is set in a school, check the network's child-directed / "designed for families" policy first: it may require non-personalized ads or rule the theme out.

## Controls

- Phone: left thumb = joystick, drag on the right side = look around, hold 🏃 to sprint
- Hide: walk into a hiding spot, or tap it when it glows
- Desktop: WASD / arrow keys to move, Shift to sprint, drag the mouse to look, E to interact, 1–4 for items (arrow keys aim, Space throws), Esc to pause

## Tuning

Every tunable number is in the `CFG` object at the top of `game.js`. Staff types, the floor plan, and obstacles are data tables just below it. Add `?debug` to the URL for an FPS counter and a 🛠 test panel.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.
